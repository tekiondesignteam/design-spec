# Templates · ARC Consumer

Ready-made templates for the **ARC Consumer** product. Organised **Template → Brand mode**; this product contributes **multiple** templates.

**Available brand modes:** Tekion · {Dealership}

## Rules

- Templates are compositions of ARC Consumer Product Kit components — no styling of their own.
- Re-theming happens by switching the underlying kit's brand mode; templates inherit it entirely.
- A template may combine components from more than one kit.
- Never style a template directly or detach kit components inside it.
